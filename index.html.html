How the EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the number of monthly instalments. In the early years most of your EMI goes to interest, which is why extra payments made early save the most.

Frequently asked questions

Does prepaying a home loan really save money?

Yes. Every extra rupee reduces the outstanding principal straight away, so you pay less interest in every later month. The earlier you prepay, the bigger the saving.

Should I reduce my EMI or my tenure when I prepay?

Reducing the tenure usually saves more interest overall. This calculator keeps your EMI fixed and shortens the tenure, which is the common choice.

Are there prepayment charges on home loans?

For floating-rate home loans to individuals, banks in India generally cannot charge a prepayment penalty. Fixed-rate loans or loans taken in a business name may have charges, so check your loan agreement.

Is this calculator exact?

It is a close estimate. Banks may round EMIs differently, change the rate during the loan, or charge fees that are not included here.
